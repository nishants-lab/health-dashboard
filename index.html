Scoring methodology

Composite of current value (60%), trend direction over the last 3 readings (25%), and a persistence penalty for consecutive out-of-range results (15%). Weights: Thyroid 20% · Lipid 25% (HDL 10%, LDL 8%, TG 7%) · Blood Sugar 15% · CBC 10% · Liver 10% · Kidney 10% · Vitamins 5% · Electrolytes 5%. Max score is 100, achievable through the right combination of medication, diet and exercise. Weights are informed by Framingham Risk Score components, ATA hypothyroidism guidelines, and LAI-2020 Indian cardiovascular risk data. This is a personal heuristic, not a diagnostic tool.

TSH (hypothyroid) - 9.07 to target <4.94

Diet + exercise + stress management, no medication
  • Selenium - 3 Brazil nuts/day (~200 µg). Supports T4 to T3 conversion and reduces thyroid antibodies
  • Zinc - pumpkin seeds (2 tbsp/day), lentils, chickpeas. Required for TSH receptor signaling
  • Iodine - iodized salt as normal in cooking; do not supplement further, excess can worsen things
  • Cook cruciferous vegetables - broccoli, cauliflower, cabbage are fine cooked; raw goitrogens interfere with iodine uptake
  • Avoid excess soy - moderate tofu is fine, do not overdo soy milk/protein
  • Gluten awareness - Anti-TPO is negative, so no Hashimoto's; gluten-free trial not needed
  • Stress management - high cortisol suppresses thyroid function; yoga, meditation, sleep quality matter
  • Exercise - moderate cardio improves thyroid hormone sensitivity; avoid over-exercising
  • Vitamin D - maintain above 40 ng/mL; deficiency correlates with thyroid autoimmunity
  • Sleep - 7 to 8 hours; TSH peaks during sleep, disrupted sleep disrupts the thyroid axis
Anti-TPO is negative, confirming this is not autoimmune. Non-autoimmune subclinical hypothyroidism responds well to lifestyle changes. Retest after 12 weeks; reconsider medication if TSH rises above 10 or symptoms worsen.

HDL - 35 to target >45

Exercise + diet + time
  • Aerobic exercise 150+ min/week - the single biggest HDL raiser (5 to 10% increase per meta-analyses)
  • Omega-3: 2 tbsp ground flaxseed/day + walnuts + algae-based DHA capsule
  • Monounsaturated fats: 1 to 2 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil daily, avocado, almonds
  • Eliminate trans fats completely (fried snacks, bakery items with vanaspati)
  • Coconut oil in moderation - raises HDL but also raises LDL
  • Lose visceral fat - even 3 to 5 kg loss improves HDL
HDL is the hardest lipid to raise through diet alone. Sustained aerobic exercise is the #1 lever; thyroid improvement adds another 5 to 15%.

LDL - 112 to target <100

Diet modification
  • Soluble fiber: 10 to 25g/day (oats, barley, beans, psyllium husk)
  • Plant sterols/stanols: 2g/day (fortified spreads or supplements)
  • Reduce saturated fat to <7% of calories (limit ghee, coconut oil, full-fat dairy)
  • Almonds - a handful (~15) daily. 5 to 7% LDL reduction (Sabate et al., JAMA 2003)
  • Soy protein 25g/day (tofu, soy milk, edamame)
  • Thyroid improvement alone can reduce LDL by 10 to 20%
Borderline LDL is achievable with diet alone if thyroid is fixed; no statin needed at current levels.

Critical safety notes - do not skip
  • Isabgol (psyllium husk): always take with at least 1 full glass (250 mL) of water immediately, then another glass within 15 to 20 min. Without enough water it can swell and block the throat or intestines. Never take it dry.
  • Isabgol timing: take 2 hours away from thyroid supplements, iron, calcium, or any medication - it blocks absorption.
  • Brazil nuts: do not eat more than 3 to 4/day. Excess selenium causes selenosis (hair loss, nail brittleness, nausea). 3 is therapeutic, 10 is toxic.
  • Flaxseed: must be ground - whole seeds pass through undigested. Store ground flaxseed in the fridge, it oxidizes quickly at room temperature.
  • Turmeric milk: always add a pinch of black pepper - without it curcumin absorption is near zero.
  • Green tea + iron: do not drink green tea with meals - tannins block iron absorption. Drink 1 hour before or after eating.
  • Water baseline: the fiber in this plan (oats, isabgol, flaxseed, beans) needs adequate hydration - minimum 3 liters/day.

Recovery

150+ min/week of moderate aerobic exercise raises HDL by 5 to 10% (Kodama et al., Archives of Internal Medicine, 2007). Zone 2 cardio - where you can hold a conversation but feel effort - is the most effective intensity for HDL. Strength training improves insulin sensitivity and lowers LDL, with less direct HDL effect than cardio.
Minimum viable version: 3 days of 45-min Zone 2 cardio (Mon/Wed/Sat) moves HDL more than any diet change alone.
Progression: weeks 1 to 4 build consistency, weeks 5 to 8 increase duration/intensity by 10%. Retest lipids after 12 weeks.
